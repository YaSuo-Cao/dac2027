# Public third-party material

The bundled PyHDL-Eval benchmark is public third-party material, not an
author identity declaration. Its original NVIDIA Research Projects MIT
license is preserved in third_party/pyhdl-eval/LICENSE. Tests, reference
Verilog, public prompts, and benchmark adapters are evaluation-only material.

The FullDocRAG corpus contains public documentation/compiler excerpts from:
PyRTL (UCSBarchlab/PyRTL), MyHDL (myhdl/myhdl), Amaranth
(amaranth-lang/amaranth), Migen (m-labs/migen), and PyMTL3 (pymtl/pymtl3).
Exact source URLs, commits/tags, archive hashes, and passage origins are in
unified_dac_experiments_840/corpus/source_manifest.json and file_inventory.json.
Original upstream license files are in third_party/notice_files/.
Public upstream copyright and license attribution is intentionally retained.
